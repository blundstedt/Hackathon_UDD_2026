LOW POLY TOWN STARTER MEGAKIT v1.1.4 - RENDER SETTINGS (sky, lights, fog, exposure, glow of the images)
=====================================================================================================
Everything you see in the images and demo scenes is a model of the pack (ground tiles, 160 m grass ground, sky dome,
neon halos, display base, vehicles). Only the light, fog, exposure and glow are engine settings: here are all their
values, so you can rebuild the same look. (Blender units: watts; Godot: energy.)

1. SKY (plain colors, like the images)
   Day:   Sky_Dome_Day_Town = palette cell sky_day_town: Classic #BFD4F3 (Blender world (0.52, 0.66, 0.90)),
          Night palette #191F38, Pastel #CEDAF6, Winter #DDE7F6. The day dome changes color with the palette, like the images.
   Night: Sky_Dome_Night_Town = palette cell sky_night_town: #191F38 in the 4 palettes (Blender world (0.010, 0.014, 0.040)).
   Domes are unlit / unshaded, at the center of the scene, radius 190 m: camera far plane 450 m or more. No shadow, no fog.
   Clouds (city_park): Cloud_Puff_A / B / C models, 58-92 m high inside the day dome.
   Ground: 3 x 3 Ground_Grass_Horizon_160m (480 m, edge hidden by the dome; city_full: scaled x 3.4, see 3.), 3 cm under the 4 m tiles (z = -0.03), centered on the dome.

2. BLENDER (Cycles, view transform "Standard", exposure 0)
   Day:   world color (0.52, 0.66, 0.90) strength 1.0; sun "Key" 3.0 W/m2 color (1.0, 0.96, 0.90) from (0.55, 0.8, -1.0)
          + sun "Fill" 1.0 color (0.78, 0.86, 1.0) from (-0.8, 0.3, -0.4), angle 8 deg. Palette emission strength 1.3.
   Night: Night palette; world color (0.010, 0.014, 0.040); both suns x 0.08, color (0.5, 0.6, 1.0);
          street lamps: point lights 160 W (1.0, 0.82, 0.55) at 4.2 m; shop neons 120 W at 2 m (see the Godot values below).
   Neon halos: material PropsHalo = palette color, emission strength 1.0, alpha 0.18.

3. GODOT 4.7 (Godot_Demo/, Pro)
   One Environment file per scene: Godot_Demo/environments/env_<scene>.tres
     background = sky color, ambient light (day 0.72/0.84/1.0 x 0.7, night 0.30/0.36/0.60 x 0.35),
     tonemap Linear, exposure 1.0, SSAO radius 1.2 intensity 1.5, glow intensity 0.4 (day) / 0.9 (night), strength 1.0,
     bloom 0.0 / 0.05, blend Additive, HDR threshold 1.0 / 0.9,
     day only: adjustments on, contrast 1.08, saturation 1.08;
     fog (night only: 0.012 night_street, 0.004 small_town_night; none by day): color = 2 x night sky color.
     Scene city_park (day): same Environment values as the other day scenes (env_city_park.tres).
      Scene city_full (day, Pro): same Environment values as the other day scenes + a DEPTH fog for a flat horizon
      (env_city_full.tres: fog_mode depth, begin 240 m, end 720 m, curve 1.0, density 1.0, color (0.765, 0.886, 0.965) =
      the rendered color of the low band of the day dome, sun scatter 0): the ground melts into the sky, no edge.
      In this scene Sky_Dome_Day_Town is scaled x 4 (height x 3.2) and the 9 Ground_Grass_Horizon_160m x 3.4 (1632 m);
      camera fov 55, distance 140, height 64, far 1300 m; 8 clouds (Cloud_Puff_A / B / C) on a 150 m ring, 57-66 m high.
   Sun: DirectionalLight3D energy 1.35 color (1.0, 0.88, 0.72), 30 deg high by day; 0.12 color (0.5, 0.6, 1.0) at night.
   Lamps (night scenes): SpotLight3D under every street lamp, energy 4.4, cone 37 deg, range 10-13 m, color (1.0, 0.94, 0.75),
     no shadow; OmniLight3D on bollard lights (0.9, range 3 m) and neon signs (1.4, range 3 m, the color of the neon).
   Palette emission energy: 1.3 by day, 2.0 at night (demo_setup.gd > emission_energy).
   prefabs/<Model>_Lit.tscn: model + halo + light, ready to use.
