AMANO PROPS - STYLE GUIDE (Town Starter MegaKit v1.1.4)
=========================================================

1. SCALE
   - 1 unit = 1 meter. Storey height 3.0 m, door 1.0 x 2.2-2.3 m, sidewalk 0.15 m high, car 1.6-1.9 m wide,
     city bus 10 m long, street lamp 4-6 m.
2. GRID
   - Road, sidewalk, parking and rail tiles: 4 x 4 m (= 2 x 2 cells of the 2 m grid = one Terrain Kit tile).
   - Walls 2 x 3 m centered on the grid line, floors 2 x 2 m (top at z = 0), Shopfront_Wide and Wall_Garage_Door
     are 4 m wide. Roof slopes span one 2 m cell and rise 1.5 m.
   - Road_Transition_Country joins a 4 m city road to the 3 m country road of the Low Poly Terrain Kit.
   - Facade kit (v1.1.1): Facade_* walls 2 x 3 m, Facade_Storefront_Big 4.1 m, Fence_Brick_Pillar sections every 2 m
     (one pillar each), Column_Classic 4 m + Portico_Pediment 6.46 m, Curb_Corner = one 4 x 4 m tile.
   - Assembly pieces (v1.1.1): walls 2 x 3 m CENTERED on the grid line (0.31 m thick), outer corners on the grid
     points, Floor_Stair_Opening = 2 x 4 m (two cells), storey height 3.0 m (the assembly guide is included in Complete / Pro).
3. ORIGINS AND ORIENTATION
   - Origin on the ground at the center of the footprint; front faces -Y in Blender.
   - Road_Straight runs along X; Road_Corner joins the +X and -Y edges; Road_T branches towards -Y;
     Road_End is entered from -X. Sidewalk curbs face -Y (Sidewalk_Corner: -Y and +X).
   - Wall items: back on the wall plane. Moving parts: separate child objects, pivot on the hinge or hub.
4. PALETTES
   - ONE texture: Textures/props_palette.png, 16 x 16 cells of 16 px (256 x 256 px).
   - Every face is UV-mapped to the CENTER of one cell: no texture painting.
   - Row 5 holds the light colors; the emission map lights them in engines that support it.
   - Classic (day), Night (dark blue, windows lit), Pastel (cosy), Winter (snow): same UVs, swap the palette AND
     the matching emission map.
   - Colors are only ever ADDED; rows 8-15 are reserved for free updates.
5. STYLE
   - Low poly, flat shaded, small bevels; 12 to 440 faces per asset in the full pack (design limit: 2,500).
   - Objects only: no characters, no drivers (tinted opaque windows), no real brands or vehicle models, no
     readable text: signs use generic pictograms (scissors, cup, burger, fuel drop, cart, cross, star...).
6. NAMING
   - Family_Variant (Road_Corner, Wall_Brick_Window, Car_Taxi, Street_Lamp_Classic...).
   - Existing file names, scales and pivots never change in updates.
